# Percent Clicker

Press any key — or a whole combo like **Ctrl+Alt+K** — automatically when you reach a chosen percentage of a level, and optionally press it again when you die.

## Features
- Unlimited **rules**, each with its own keys, percentage, hold time and name
- **Key combos**: `ctrl+alt+k`, `shift+f5`, `space`, arrows, F-keys, numpad…
- **Hold time** in milliseconds (0 = quick tap)
- **Repeat every X%** after the first press
- **When I die**: do nothing, press the moment you die, or press when the attempt restarts (with an optional delay)
- **Play mode filter**: any mode, normal only, or practice only
- Works with checkpoints in practice mode (rules "behind" your respawn point won't fire instantly)
- Edit everything from the **Percent Clicker button in the pause menu**
- Global on/off keybind (default **F8**) in the mod settings

## How to use
1. Pause a level and click the Percent Clicker button (bottom right).
2. Press **New**, fill in the keys and percentage, press **Save**.
3. Resume — the keys are pressed for you at that percentage.

Because the mod sends real key events, you can trigger keybinds from other mods too.
